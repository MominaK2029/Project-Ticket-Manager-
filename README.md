This project was inspired by my schools Electrical Program. Most of our schools 'shops' work for the school itself and the city. Electrical helps us with anything 
related to wires and the lights in the school. They fix our clocks, and many different things around the building. The way they organize it, was a problem. Electrical
uses a google form to get orders and a google sheet to organize. This created a very messy environment for their work and seem way to stressful to go through.
I'm creating a project that will help organize their tasks by tabs and charts that can filter out everything and make their lives so much easier. It's a basic kind of 
website that I hope they will like and grow used too, making their lives easier.
